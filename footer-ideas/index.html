Graphyte Footer Ideas

Three footers with the hex pattern

All three use the same hex lattice as the hero and the generator, drawn live here so you can resize the window and see how they hold up. Every one can be built on the Relume footer with a CSS background on the section, so none of them needs a custom component.

1 · Ink bloom

The safe pick. The hero's bloom returns at the bottom of the page in the dark scheme, so the site opens and closes on the same mark. Orange cells on ink, dense in the corner, gone by the CTA. The CTA card on the homepage already sits in this scheme, so it reads as one dark band.

Relume side: footer section on Scheme 3 (ink), background image set to a dark corner SVG from the generator, anchored bottom right, no repeat.

2 · Horizon

The quiet one. Light footer, and the hexes form a horizon along the bottom edge rather than a corner: ink cells fading upward, with the last few rows going orange where they meet the page edge. The copyright line floats on the band in a small pill. Calmer than the dark options, and it keeps the page light all the way down.

3 · Stamp

The bold one. The CTA becomes an orange card stamped over the top edge of a dark footer, with the hex bloom inside the card in ink at low opacity. The pattern lives in the one place you want eyes to land, and the links sit quietly underneath. Loudest of the three, and the most "Graphyte" if you want the orange to carry the page out.
