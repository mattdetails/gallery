Graphyte Gallery

Tools, templates and design experiments from Graphyte, a small web studio. Each one started as a working page built with Claude. These are static snapshots, so a few features that needed a live account are switched off or use example data.

Tools

Hex Generator

Shape a graphite hexagon lattice with sliders, watch it fade by cell size, and copy it as SVG.

Post Maker

Drop in a screenshot and write one line to get a branded 1080×1350 image for LinkedIn and Instagram.

Right-click the preview to save it

Pricing Calculator

Services, complexity, risk and timeline turn into a floor-to-stretch price range with a value check.

Runs on example numbers

Share Image Picker

Three 1200×630 share images, each previewed as a LinkedIn, X, iMessage and chat-app card.

Templates and frameworks

Web Design Master Map

A web design process from intro call to launch, drawn as a transit map, with separate views for the client, the team and the agents.

Client Questionnaire

A six-part questionnaire to send before kickoff. Blank, "not sure" and vague answers become the agenda for the kickoff call.

Client View

A project board for clients with their tasks, what needs their review, what's next and what got done this week.

Example project

Messaging House

A plain-English guide to messaging houses, positioning tools and voice, and how to turn them into a copy pipeline.

Design experiments

Footer Ideas

Three footers built on a hex lattice drawn live in the browser: Ink bloom, Horizon and Stamp.

The r Button

A glowing map toggle shown four ways (matte, lit, ringed and liquid orb), with a safe-area overlay and optional sound.

At home

Recipe Box

A family meal planner. Vote on recipes over six rounds, keep the ones you like, and set which basics to buy each week.
